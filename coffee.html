🗓️Every day, a adventure is different

Monday: Italian Night

Start the week with flavor: homemade pasta dishes accompanied by wine or Fernet in a warm and delicious evening.

Tuesday: Parlana

A night to practice languages, make new friends, and connect with the world without leaving the hostel. All accents are welcome!

Wednesday: Bolivian Night

Discover the flavors and traditions of Bolivia with typical dishes, local drinks, and good company. Culture in every bite.

Thursday: Bachata & Peceras

Learn to move to the rhythm of bachata and enjoy our peceras (large drinks to share). Rhythm and fun guaranteed!

Friday: Karaoke + Ladies Night

Sing your favorite songs, celebrate Friday, and enjoy special drinks for ladies. A night of laughter, voices, and party!

Saturday: Pizza Rodizio

Eat all you want: assorted pizzas served non-stop on a delicious night perfect for sharing with new friends.

Sunday: Latin American and Bolivian Cinema

We close the week with cinema and culture. Relax with good movies, popcorn, and a deep look at our identity.
