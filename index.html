We are delighted to welcome you to our community of Spanish learners. Here, you will learn the language in a practical, dynamic, and personalized way while discovering the cultural and historical richness of Sucre, the beautiful “White City” of Bolivia.

In our school, you will not only find high-quality classes with passionate teachers, but also a friendly space where you can live the Spanish language every moment: in the classroom, through cultural activities, and in daily life with local families and fellow students from around the world.

More than just a school, we are a place to share experiences, make friends, and feel at home while achieving your goals with the language.

“Welcome to SpanishLessonsBolivia, your home away from home, where you’ll learn authentic Latin American Spanish with excellent native teachers and immerse yourself in Bolivia’s vibrant culture.”

Homestay (Host Family)

The student will have a private room within the host family’s home. Two meals per day are included: breakfast and lunch. Dinner is left open so that the student can either prepare something at home or enjoy dining out in the city. The homestay experience offers an authentic cultural immersion, where the student can share daily life, customs, and local traditions with the family. In addition, it provides the opportunity to practice Spanish at all times in a warm and welcoming environment.

Hostel

Another option is to stay in our hostel, ideal for those who prefer a youthful, dynamic environment with greater independence. You can choose between:

  • Private Room: Designed for students seeking more privacy, comfort, and tranquility.
  • Shared Room: A more affordable and social option, perfect for meeting travelers from different countries.

The hostel includes common areas, a shared kitchen, internet, and social spaces, making it easy to interact with other travelers while enjoying life in the city.
