Tour Minas - Refineria y Mercado minerales

The MINAS tour offers a unique opportunity to explore the mineral wealth of Potosi. Visit the refining facilities and the bustling mineral market to learn about the region's rich mining history.

Description of the Tour

An extraordinary tour where visitors can explore the mines of Cerro Rico in Potosí. Our itinerary begins with picking up the guest from their accommodation, followed by visits to various sites such as the mining market, the mineral refinery, the interior of the mine, and the working areas within. The tour also includes a visit to "El Tío," the mine’s deity, accompanied by many stories and anecdotes about these places. All of this will be guided by an experienced former miner who worked in these mines for many years.

INCA TRAIL - MARAGUA CRATER - DEVIL`S THROAT

A unique adventure awaits you on the Inca Trail, where breathtaking landscapes and rich cultural heritage converge.

Description of the Tour

Our tour begins at the Colors hostel and you will go to the Chataquila church which is at 3650 meters where the trekking of approximately 2 hours along the Inca trail will begin. At the end of the road your private transportation will pick you up to go to the Maragua Crater where you will visit the Devil's Throat and other tourist sites in the area and then enjoy your box lunch and return to Sucre in the afternoon.
